1.5.1

# 1.5.1

- No flashing on your action bars while you're mounted. The HUD still shows what's next; the
  flashes come back as soon as you dismount. Missing buff reminders also wait until you dismount.
- All action-button flashes pulse in step: the red missing-buff flash pulses together with the
  rotation flash, so you can pick at a glance whether to attack or buff.
- In a party or raid, your raid buff (Fortitude, Kings, Mark of the Wild, Brilliance, Dark Intent,
  shouts, Horn of Winter...) is also checked on everyone near you. If someone in range is missing
  it (and has no equivalent buff), the reminder shows; hover it to see who.

# 1.5.0

Missing buff reminders and name-only nameplates.

- Missing buffs show as a red-edged bar just above the main HUD icon, as wide as the icon and
  split between the missing buffs, and their buttons
  on your action bars flash bright red. A reminder goes away as soon as you have the buff, or the
  same kind of buff from someone else (Mark of the Wild, Blessing of Kings and Legacy of the
  Emperor all count as the stats buff, Battle Shout and Trueshot Aura count as Horn of Winter...).
  On MoP the game's own raid buff tracking decides what's covered. Hover an icon for its name.
- Every MoP class: raid buffs you provide, plus Death Knight presence, Druid Moonkin Form (in
  combat), Hunter aspect and pet, Mage armor, Monk stance, Paladin seal and Righteous Fury, Priest
  Inner Fire and Shadowform, Rogue poisons, Shaman shield and weapon imbues, Warlock pet (not with
  Grimoire of Sacrifice) and Warrior shouts.
- Seal, presence, Horn of Winter and Inner Fire moved out of the rotation into these reminders.
- Turn them off with /rcr buffs or Options > General > HUD.
- Name-only enemy nameplates: RCRotations counts enemies for AoE through enemy nameplates, so with
  nameplates off it always thinks there's one target. The new option (Options > Extra, or
  /rcr plates) turns enemy nameplates on and hides their health bars: you still see enemy names
  and cast bars. Works with Blizzard's nameplates and with ElvUI's (there the name stays centered,
  and the health %, level, target glow and highlight hide with the bar). Your old setting comes back when you turn it off. If nameplates are off, you get
  a one-time tip about it.

# 1.4.3

- Locking the HUD sticks: once you lock it, it stays locked on every login and on all your
  characters, and they all share the same HUD position. Unlock it with the new "Unlock HUD" button
  at the top of the menu (/rcr), next to Role.

# 1.4.2

WoW Forever gets rotations again.

- WoWSims has no WoW Forever sim, so on Forever RCRotations uses the original Forever Rotation
  lists (by Vohnka) as the built-in rotation for every class and role, the way Forever Rotation
  did. Everywhere else RCRotations stays WoWSims-only.
- A rotation you import or save from the Workshop app replaces the built-in list on Forever.
  /rcr options > Rotation shows which one is in use.
- Workshop app 1.2.0: the Forever Rotation lists show up under each class as "WoW Forever",
  ready to tweak and save, and you can build a new rotation for WoW Forever too. The app also
  finds Forever-style game folders (AddOns right inside the game folder).

# 1.4.1

- Discipline Priest has a rotation ("Atonement"): Shadow Word: Pain, Penance, Holy Fire or Power
  Word: Solace, Archangel at 5 Evangelism, Mindbender or Shadowfiend for mana, Smite as filler and
  Mind Sear on 4+ targets. WoWSims has no Discipline rotation, so this one is ours.
- HUD: the main icon always shows; the two next-spell icons only appear when there's a next spell.
  The empty icon is a centered skull without the frame lines.
- Workshop app 1.1.0: a new "Build a new rotation" tab. Pick your class and spec, drag spells into
  your rotation, add conditions from a list (buff missing, debuff under N seconds, resource,
  target health, number of targets, moving and more), then Save to WoW.

# 1.4.0

Smarter rotations for real play, a cleaner HUD and the Workshop app.

- Rotations are tuned for real play on top of WoWSims. Simulators assume you did some things before
  the pull, so RCRotations now reminds you in combat:
  - Retribution: a seal when you have none (Seal of Truth, or Righteousness on 4+ targets).
  - Death Knight: the right presence, Horn of Winter when no attack power buff is up, and for Blood,
    Bone Shield upkeep and Death Strike first at 40% health or lower.
  - Warlock: Curse of the Elements when nobody has the 5% magic damage debuff on the target.
- Demonology Metamorphosis: the HUD shows Touch of Chaos, Doom, Chaos Wave and Soul Fire to match
  your bars, never suggests Metamorphosis while it's up, and shows a red CANCEL label when it's time
  to end it.
- Unholy: Dark Transformation waits for 5 Shadow Infusion stacks.
- Coming up: when fewer than three spells are ready, the HUD shows the next spells coming off
  cooldown with their timer instead of going blank. /rcr soon turns it off.
- The Damage / PvE toolbar under the HUD is gone. Role and profile are in the menu (/rcr).
- RCRotations Workshop, a free Windows app: edit any WoWSims rotation and save it straight into the
  addon (it shows in your rotation list as "(Workshop)"). Download:
  https://github.com/Rawcard/RCRotations/releases/tag/workshop (also in /rcr options > Rotation,
  or /rcr workshop).
- Pasting a NAG rotation into /rcr import now explains that only WoWSims rotations import.

# 1.3.0

WoWSims rotations on MoP Classic.

- Every MoP class gets real rotations, generated from WoWSims (wowsims/mop, MIT): 73 rotations
  covering all DPS and tank specs plus Holy Priest. The rotation follows your
  current specialization automatically.
- Rotation picker: when your spec has more than one WoWSims rotation (Frost DK: Obliterate or
  Masterfrost), a window asks on login or spec change. The choice is saved per character and spec
  until you change it with /rcr apl.
- Import rotations in game: Options > Rotation > "Import WoWSims" (or /rcr import, or the picker's
  "Import a rotation..." button). Paste a rotation exported from wowsims.com; it's converted on
  the spot, saved for all your characters under the current spec, and shown as "(imported)" in the
  picker. The X next to it removes it.
- Rotations update through the addon: a daily GitHub job pulls WoWSims changes into a pull request.
- Simulator-only conditions (trinket procs, boss timers, scripted sequences) use stand-in values;
  everything else (auras, dots, cooldowns, runes, chi, holy power, energy...) is read live.
- WoWSims only: the built-in rotation lists, their editor and the Auto/AoE/Burst buttons are
  gone. On Era, TBC and Forever you import a WoWSims rotation per class; on first login with no
  rotation for your class/spec, a window offers the import. The Options Rotation tab shows the
  active WoWSims rotation with Choose and Import buttons. Defense, interrupt, purge, cleanse and
  weapon reminders are unchanged.
- Flashes: any bar addon is detected (ElvUI, Bartender, Dominos, RCDiabloUI, or anything built on
  LibActionButton). Only visible buttons flash, and a flash hides while its button is hidden,
  so there are no stray flashes where a bar addon hid Blizzard's buttons.
- /rcr why lists the suggestions and the buttons each one flashes.
- New default HUD style "Compact": a big square main icon with a thin gold edge, no panel behind
  it, a large keybind in the bottom-left and a small RC badge, with the next two spells smaller
  beside it, all with black borders. Switch back with Options > General > Display ("HUD: Classic")
  or /rcr style.
- Compact style replaces the padlock with a thin "Click to lock" bar under the icons. It disappears
  once locked; unlock from Options > General > Lock (or the minimap button).
- The RC badge on the main icon is a button: click it to open the options.
- The defense, interrupt, purge, cleanse and weapon-buff windows are off by default (switched off
  once on existing characters too). Turn them on in Options > General > Windows.
- Suggestion icons stay clickable while locked (right-click = never suggest); dragging still
  needs the HUD unlocked.
- Spells the game says you can't cast right now (Dark Transformation without 5 Shadow Infusion,
  procs, forms, resources) are never suggested.
- Never-suggest list, applied on top of every rotation so WoWSims updates can't bring them back:
  personal defensives and raid cooldowns by default (Anti-Magic Shell, Icebound Fortitude,
  Army of the Dead, Bloodlust/Heroism/Time Warp, ...). Right-click a spell on the HUD to add it;
  /rcr unblock <spell> to remove; /rcr blocked to list; Options > Rotation shows it with a reset.

# 1.2.0

Runs on every Classic client, not just Forever.

- One folder loads on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary, Wrath, Cata and MoP Classic, plus WoW Forever.
  Retail is excluded on purpose (Midnight hides combat state from addons).
- New Compat.lua detects the client (ns.GAME: vanilla, tbc, wrath, cata, mists, forever).
- Death Knight and Monk recognized: class colors, roles (DK damage/tank, Monk damage/tank/heal), HUD.
  They have no rotation lists yet, so the HUD stays empty for them until the WoWSims import lands.
- Interrupts and purges updated per client: Wind Shear, Mind Freeze, Rebuke, Skull Bash,
  Counter Shot, Spear Hand Strike, and MoP's Dispel Magic ID.
- Energy-tick and swing-clip timing only runs on Era, TBC and Forever (later clients don't have them).
- Rotation lists are still the Classic Era lists. Spells resolve by name, so they mostly work on TBC;
  on Wrath, Cata and MoP they're a placeholder until the WoWSims rotations are imported.

# 1.1.0

RC SpellFlash built in.

- New default flash style ported from RC-SpellFlash 2.1.1: colored glow fill plus a pulsing outline,
  drawn above the button so hidden bar headers (RCDiabloUI) can't hide it.
- Colors: next spell yellow, defense/long buffs cyan, interrupt orange, purge purple,
  heals and weapon imbues keep their own colors.
- Switch styles in Options > General > Display ("RC SpellFlash style"), or with /rcr flash.
  Turning it off brings back the original skull.
- More bars detected (from RC-SpellFlash): ElvUI stance bar, pet action bar, RCDiabloUI bars and stance bar.

# 1.0.0

First RCRotations release, forked from Forever Rotation 1.5.72 by Vohnka (MIT).

- Renamed to RCRotations: folder, TOC files, title, author, category.
- New slash commands: /rcr (also /rcrot and /rcrotations). Credit toggle: /rcrmsg on|off.
- New SavedVariables names (RCRotationsDB, RCRotationsCharDB, RCRotationsCreditDB) and frame names,
  so RCRotations can be installed next to the original without conflicts.
- Info panel and login line credit Vohnka; links point to the original project for reference.
- Rotation logic, holds, profiles and HUD behaviour are unchanged from Forever Rotation 1.5.72.
- Profile share strings keep the original format, so strings from Forever Rotation still import.
